Yes, this project is being developed with the help of AI, mainly ChatGPT.

I am not a professional emulator developer, reverse engineer, compiler engineer, audio engineer, graphics programmer, or whatever other kind of wizard you probably need to be to properly understand everything happening here.

In fact, a significant part of this project can basically be summarized as:

Me: “I want Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock on PC to look and behave like the Xbox 360 version.”
ChatGPT: “Alright, this is going to get weird.”

Why?

Because I simply wanted to play Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock with the visual quality and overall presentation of the Xbox 360 version.

I personally don't like how the PS3 version looks, and apparently that minor complaint somehow evolved into modifying runtimes, debugging XMA audio, investigating Xbox 360 behavior, touching GPU code, building custom tools, staring at hexadecimal values for hours, and accidentally creating an entire compatibility project.

Do I fully understand everything I'm doing?

Absolutely not... or yes xD.

Am I learning things along the way?

Somehow, yes.

Does the game work better because of it?

That's the important part.

You can question my methods. You cannot question my results. 😎

GHWoR-ReXGlue
Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock PC Compatibility Project

GHWoR-ReXGlue is a community-driven project focused on improving compatibility and runtime behavior of Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock (Xbox 360) on PC through a ReXGlue-based runtime environment.

The goal is to provide a more accurate and stable experience while preserving the original game's behavior, including music timing, gameplay systems and Xbox 360 features.
